Sources and method

  • CapnBry's Bestiary (capnbry.net/daoc): radar captures from 2002–2004, names and level ranges per zone, plus sighting coordinates. The primary period source here.
  • Illia's Camelot Bestiary (Allakhazam's DAoC bestiary): per-zone monster lists and level ranges collected from players over many years; used to confirm CapnBry and to cover zones CapnBry lacks.
  • Uthgard 2.0 (disorder.dk): a classic freeshard's kill database. Its species lists are used as a third opinion only; its levels and battleground brackets were changed from live and are not used.
  • Left out: Trials of Atlantis zones and the post-1.80 New Frontiers zones (the server runs Classic and Shrouded Isles with the old frontiers), player pets, mounts, boats and holiday invasion monsters seen by radar.
  • Server data: every realm-less monster spawn in the Mob table, grouped by zone; levels from the spawn's template range where it has one.
